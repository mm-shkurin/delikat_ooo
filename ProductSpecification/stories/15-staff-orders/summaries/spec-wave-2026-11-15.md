# Spec wave — story 15

### 2026-11-15 — status chain replaces the mock's free select
- Type: decision
- Note: Staff may only move accepted → packing|cancelled, packing → ready|cancelled, ready → done|cancelled. Done and cancelled are terminal. The mock select that offers every status is not the prod rule. Same status again is 200. A forbidden step is 409 «Этот статус отсюда не поставить.»

### 2026-11-15 — no phone; name and email are a checkout snapshot
- Type: surprise
- Note: Orders have no phone. The shop cart is not tied to an account. Guest cards read «Гость». A live Bearer on POST /orders stores the trimmed storefront display_name (not provider_display_name) and the account email once. Replay and a later profile edit do not rewrite the snapshot. OrderCreated and the receipt do not gain those fields. A bad Bearer is 401 and must not place a guest order.

### 2026-11-15 — city stays in JSON and off the card
- Type: quirk
- Note: fulfillment.address.city is almost always Омск, so search and the card ignore it. Search is client-side over the full list: order id, first 8 characters, name, email, note, street. Trim the query; whitespace-only matches everything. Contact with email is `{email} · № {first 8}`; without email it is `№ {first 8}`.

### 2026-11-15 — pill colors and session copy
- Type: invariant
- Note: `--text` and `--solid` are the same hex, so packing uses `--accent`. Ready uses `--success` and cancelled `--danger` as text on `--surface` even though those pairs are missing from tokens.pairs. No fill. Signed-out and non-staff stay on the story 12 gate. «Сессия истекла. Войдите заново.» is only for a list or status save that dies on an already open tab, and for a dead checkout in the cart error slot. The receipt opens only after 201.
